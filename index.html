CASE STUDY 01 // 3D COMBAT The Cowboy
Tactical Shooter
UNITY 3D · C#

The Cowboy

Tactical 3D top-down shooter featuring mixed-threat squad AI, vehicular sandbox mechanics, and timed extraction pressure

A fast-paced 3D top-down shooter built around high-stakes combat and dynamic sandbox encounters. Engineered in Unity using modern C# architecture, players must out-maneuver coordinated AI archetypes, weaponize drivable vehicles, and complete time-critical evacuation objectives across desert canyon bottlenecks.

Encounter Design Sightline Architecture AI Squad Composition Resource Economy Unity New Input System
  • Choke Point & Sightline Architecture: Structured canyon bottlenecks and angled vehicle wrecks to create intentional line-of-sight breaks and cover-to-cover flanking routes.
  • Pacing & Resource Pressure: Coupled a strict evacuation countdown timer with finite ammo drops, forcing players to trade safe defensive play for aggressive forward pushes.
  • Dynamic Encounter Compositions: Combined close-range melee rushers with cover-seeking riflemen to punish static corner-camping and demand constant spatial repositioning.
  • Vehicular Sandbox Utility: Integrated drivable vehicles as dual-purpose traversal tools and mobile cover/battering rams to breach heavily fortified extraction zones.
Play Build on itch.io

Playtest Finding & Iteration

Problem: Early playtesters camped behind the initial canyon entry rocks and picked off AI one-by-one.
Design Fix: Introduced Aggressive Rusher AI flankers, tightened ammo drops on passive kills, and tuned the extraction clock to reward forward momentum.

Key Balancing Levers (C#)

Exposed modular inspector variables for EvacTimerDuration, AI_DetectionRadius, RusherFlankSpeed, and VehicleRamDamageMultiplier for rapid encounter tuning.

CASE STUDY 02 // UE5 & SYSTEMS Street Dash:
Hyperdelivery UE5 Blueprints · UMG · Route Loop
UNREAL ENGINE 5 · BLUEPRINTS & UMG

Street Dash: Hyperdelivery

High-velocity arcade delivery systems design & UE5 prototype featuring risk/reward route optimization, combo multipliers, and telemetry HUD

An arcade action-delivery concept and technical systems prototype built around high-speed urban navigation and split-second route optimization. Designed to test how spatial risk, momentum conservation, and dynamic delivery timers create an addictive "one-more-run" score-chasing core loop in Unreal Engine 5.

UE5 Blueprints UMG Widget UI Core Loop & Economy Route Optimization 3Cs & Arcade Feel
  • Multi-Tiered Route Architecture: Designed intersecting level lanes (Safe Main Roads vs. High-Risk Alley Shortcuts) that test player map mastery and momentum control under decaying delivery clocks.
  • Momentum & Combo Systems: Engineered a flow-state scoring loop where chaining near-misses, drift turns, and on-time drop-offs scales a dynamic payout multiplier while cargo damage resets streaks.
  • Real-Time UMG Telemetry & Feedback: Structured non-intrusive UMG HUD widgets and directional objective indicators so players can read timer states and route branches at high speeds without eye-strain.
  • Dynamic Difficulty & Pinch-Point Balancing: Modeled escalation curves across shift waves by scaling traffic density, fragile-package modifiers, and multi-stop prioritization puzzles.
Request GDD & Blueprint Walkthrough

Core Loop Architecture

Accept Dispatch → Select Route Risk (Safe vs. Shortcut) → Maintain Speed/Combo Multiplier → Execute Drop-off → Invest Payout in Handling/Cargo Upgrades.

UE5 Technical Implementation

Built modular Blueprint splines for route checkpoints, dynamic UMG widget animations bound to timer decay events, and physical camera FOV scaling tied to player velocity.

CASE STUDY 03 // 3Cs & PACING Tilevania
3Cs & Level Study
UNITY 2D · C# · 3Cs TUNING

Tilevania — 2D Movement & Level Pacing Study

Modular 2D platformer focused on sub-frame jump responsiveness (coyote time / input buffering), sightline readability, and risk/reward routing

A technical level design and 3Cs movement feel study built with Unity's Tilemap ecosystem and the New Input System. Engineered multi-tiered traversal rooms using Composite Colliders and Rule Tiles, custom-tuned physics curves for crisp platforming, and persistent singleton state managers across level portals.

3Cs Movement Feel Coyote Time & Buffering Hazard Cadence Spatial Readability Cinemachine Confiners
  • Micro-Pacing & Jump Precision: Fine-tuned coyote time, jump input buffering, and custom gravity multipliers on the Rigidbody2D to eliminate floatiness and deliver responsive, skill-testing traversal.
  • Hazard Rhythm & Readability: Arranged spike traps, moving hazards, and enemy patrols using strict visual contrast and rhythmic spacing—teaching mechanics safely before stacking difficulty.
  • Risk/Reward Coin Routing: Placed optional high-value coin clusters along tight platform edges and enemy patrol loops, creating micro-decisions between safe completion and speedrun mastery.
  • Session Continuity Architecture: Scripted persistent session singletons in C# to retain life tallies, score states, and item pickups seamlessly across scene transitions.
Play Build on itch.io

Playtest Finding & Iteration

Problem: Players frequently missed ledge jumps by 2–3 frames on descending platforms and blamed input lag.
Design Fix: Added a 0.12s coyote-time grace window, 0.15s jump input buffer, and increased fall gravity scale to make landings snappy and predictable.

Level Pacing Framework

Structured each stage around the 4-step Kishōtenketsu cadence: Introduce mechanic in safe zone → Develop with movement → Twist by combining enemy patrol + spikes → Conclude at portal checkpoint.
